# Legal Framework

## Purpose

This document records the verified legal, policy, and institutional sources that form the SafeSpace knowledge base.

SafeSpace should not generate legal information from general model knowledge where no verified source exists.

## Trusted Support Services

Potential support pathways include:

### Child Helpline

Service:
Child Helpline 116

Purpose:
Child protection support, counselling, referrals, and escalation.

### Gender-Based Violence Support

Service:
GBV Helpline 1195

Purpose:
Support and referral for gender-based violence cases.

### Legal Aid

Institution:
National Legal Aid Service

Purpose:
Legal aid and legal support for eligible users.

### Police Oversight

Institution:
Independent Policing Oversight Authority

Purpose:
Complaints related to police misconduct.

## Legal Content Rule

Every SafeSpace rights record should include:

- title
- topic
- jurisdiction
- source
- section or article reference where available
- plain-language explanation
- appropriate next step
- support pathway
- last verification date
- status

## Verification Status

Possible status values:

VERIFIED

REVIEW_REQUIRED

EXPIRED

ARCHIVED

SafeSpace should not present EXPIRED or ARCHIVED information as current guidance.
