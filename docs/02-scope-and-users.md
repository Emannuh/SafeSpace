# Scope and Users

## Primary User

The initial SafeSpace user is a young person in Kenya seeking understandable and trustworthy rights information.

The MVP is designed with particular attention to minors and young people who may be experiencing vulnerable or high-risk situations.

## Secondary Users

The platform may also be useful to:

- parents and guardians
- teachers
- community workers
- youth advocates
- social workers
- trusted adults supporting young people

However, the MVP design prioritises the young person.

## MVP Journeys

### Journey 1: Teenage Pregnancy and Education Rights

Example user question:

"I am under 18 and pregnant. What are my rights, and can I continue or return to school?"

The system should help the user understand:

- education-related rights
- re-entry pathways
- relevant policy
- appropriate support services
- next steps

### Journey 2: Sexual Exploitation and Protection

Example user question:

"Someone older is sexually exploiting or abusing me. What protections do I have, and who can I safely tell?"

The system should help the user understand:

- whether the described behaviour may fall within a protected or unlawful category
- what rights may apply
- where to seek protection
- which trusted services are available
- what to do if there is immediate danger

### Journey 3: Rights of a Child Accused of an Offence

Example user question:

"I am under 18 and police say I committed an offence. What are my rights?"

The system should help explain:

- rights during arrest
- right to legal representation
- presence of a parent or guardian where applicable
- detention safeguards
- fair trial protections
- legal aid pathways
- appropriate reporting channels if misconduct is alleged

## MVP Boundaries

SafeSpace will not:

- determine guilt or innocence
- give personalised legal representation
- replace a lawyer
- investigate offences
- collect evidence on behalf of the user
- guarantee legal outcomes
- act as an emergency-response authority
- diagnose medical conditions

## Geographic Scope

MVP jurisdiction:

Kenya

The system architecture will be designed so that additional jurisdictions can later be introduced through separate legal and institutional knowledge packs.

## Language Scope

MVP:

- English
- Kiswahili demonstration

Future architecture may support additional languages.
